# User Stories

Convert backlog ideas into user stories including titles and short descriptions.
Each story includes a priority and effort estimate in days, then is assigned to Iteration 1 or 2.

Story files are located in: [./user_stories](./user_stories)

| ID | Title | Description | Priority (10/20/30) | Estimate (days) | Iteration | Story File |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| US-01 | Browse restaurants | As a customer, I want to see a list of restaurants so I can choose where to order from. | 10 | 1 | 1 | [link](./user_stories/user_story_01_browse_restaurants.md) |
| US-02 | View restaurant menu | As a customer, I want to view a restaurant’s menu so I can decide what to buy. | 10 | 1 | 1 | [link](./user_stories/user_story_02_view_menu.md) |
| US-03 | Add items to cart | As a customer, I want to add menu items to a cart so I can prepare an order. | 10 | 1 | 1 | [link](./user_stories/user_story_03_add_to_cart.md) |
| US-04 | Update cart quantities | As a customer, I want to change quantities or remove items so my cart matches what I want. | 20 | 1 | 1 | [link](./user_stories/user_story_04_update_cart.md) |
| US-05 | Place an order | As a customer, I want to place an order with delivery details so the restaurant receives my request. | 10 | 2 | 1 | [link](./user_stories/user_story_05_place_order.md) |
| US-06 | Restaurant manages menu | As a restaurant, I want to create and edit menu items so customers can order accurately. | 20 | 2 | 1 | [link](./user_stories/user_story_06_manage_menu.md) |

| US-09 | Track order status | As a customer, I want to see my order status so I know what’s happening. | 20 | 2 | 2 | [link](./user_stories/user_story_09_track_order_status.md) |
| US-10 | Driver accepts a delivery | As a driver, I want to accept an available delivery so I can deliver an order. | 30 | 1 | 2 | [link](./user_stories/user_story_10_driver_accept_delivery.md) |

## Iteration totals
- Iteration 1 total estimate: 10 days (US-01..US-08)
- Iteration 2 total estimate: 3 days (US-09..US-10)
